Privacy Protocol

Effective Date: June 15, 2024

CHERRY DENTAL DESIGN LIMITED strictly enforces data protection standards, particularly given the sensitive nature of clinical environments. This document details our data handling procedures for our enterprise applications.

1. Diagnostic Collection

We do not passively extract patient or personal data. Our enterprise CAD applications log anonymized crash reports and engine metrics solely to refine 3D rendering stability and reduce latency.

2. Information Processing

Information actively supplied by users through our B2B support terminals or beta registration forms is processed exclusively to deliver technical support and authenticate corporate access.

Operating out of Nottingham, United Kingdom, our protocols align strictly with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). Corporate clients retain full authority to request audits, modifications, or erasure of their records via our support channels.
